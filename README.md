# Sales_analytics_dashboard
Sales Analytics Dashboard using Tableau for sales performance and business insights
## 🔗 Live Dashboard

View the interactive Tableau dashboard here:

[👉 Sales Analytics Dashboard](https://prod-in-a.online.tableau.com/#/site/ritikmukhiya88-19c1ef762a/views/SalesAnalyticsDashboard/SALESANALYTICSDASHBOARD?:iid=3)
## 📊 Dashboard Features

- Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Orders KPI monitoring
- Monthly Sales Trend analysis
- Sales performance by Region
- Sales performance by Category
- Sales analysis by Customer Segment
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Interactive filters for Region, Category, Segment, and Order Date
- Executive-friendly dashboard for quick business decision-making

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Tableau** — Interactive dashboard and data visualization
- **SQL** — Sales data analysis and business queries
- **Python** — Data analysis and preprocessing
- **GitHub** — Version control and project documentation

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Technology is the strongest-performing product category by sales.
- West and East regions contribute significantly to overall sales.
- Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments can be compared for customer-level opportunities.
- Monthly sales show noticeable fluctuations, highlighting stronger and weaker periods.
- Top-performing products can be used to identify major revenue contributors.
