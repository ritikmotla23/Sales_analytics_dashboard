# Sales Analytics Dashboard - Project Report

## 1. Project Overview

The Sales Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Tableau dashboard developed to analyze sales performance and generate meaningful business insights.

The project uses the Sample Superstore dataset and focuses on sales, profit, orders, regional performance, product performance, categories, and customer segments.

## 2. Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify monthly sales trends
- Compare regional sales performance
- Analyze sales by product category
- Identify top-performing products
- Understand customer segment performance
- Provide an interactive business intelligence dashboard

## 3. Tools & Technologies

- Tableau
- SQL
- Python
- Pandas
- GitHub
- CSV Dataset

## 4. Key KPIs

- Total Sales: 2,297,201
- Total Profit: 286,397
- Total Orders: 5,009

## 5. Dashboard Analysis

### Monthly Sales Trend

The dashboard shows monthly sales fluctuations and helps identify high-performing and low-performing periods.

### Regional Analysis

Sales are compared across Central, East, South, and West regions to identify stronger and weaker markets.

### Category Analysis

The dashboard analyzes Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology categories.

### Customer Segment Analysis

Sales performance is analyzed across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

### Top Products

The dashboard identifies the top 10 products based on sales.

## 6. Business Insights

- Technology is one of the strongest product categories.
- The West region shows strong sales performance.
- Sales vary significantly across months.
- Customer segments contribute differently to overall sales.
- Top products can be targeted for future sales and marketing strategies.

## 7. Conclusion

The Sales Analytics Dashboard provides an interactive view of business performance and enables users to identify sales trends, regional opportunities, product performance, and customer behavior.

The project demonstrates the practical use of Tableau, SQL, Python, and GitHub in a data analytics workflow.
